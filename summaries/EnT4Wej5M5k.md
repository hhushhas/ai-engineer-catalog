## Gist

I built evaluation systems at Whimo and Uber ATG for salt driving cars, which are really the OG AI agents and for rockets at SpaceX.

## Why Hasan Should Care

This session maps to general ai engineering. For Hasan's work, the useful lens is whether it changes how to build, evaluate, ship, or operate AI-enabled products and agent workflows (11:20).

## Practical Takeaways

- A model you're testing, a set of questions we call the test set, and a metric, how we keep the score.
- And the key insight is that benchmarks standardize the test set and metrics across these models.
- The company funding the benchmark is to see all the questions, evaluate their models internally, and announce their scores before there's independent verification.
- When benchmark creators take money from the companies they're evaluating, it undermines the whole system.
- We need to open source the data and have methodologies and clo code with no financial ties between the benchmark creators and the model companies.
