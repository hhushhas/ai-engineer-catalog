## Practical Takeaways

- We built a hypothesis research system for hypothesis generation in drug discovery.
- So Bayer team came to us to ask for an agentic system that would allow them to build and iterate on the corpus of research they already have to predict the areas where their scientists should focus.
- There was just too much data and they needed a memory system to pinpoint and locate where is the interesting area for research for their agentic workflows.
- The Bayer team needed to effectively understand in which area the hypothesis generation and the scientific discovery should continue and in which area it's not relevant.
- And for to do that, Cogni's team needed to understand together with Bayer very specific domain of biomedical research, iterate on it, validate it, and then predict the area of where this future improvements can happen.
