## Gist

So, the thing I want to talk about is basically like even with the advent of like very good AI tools today, like the principles of good design hasn't really changed.

## Why Hasan Should Care

This session maps to general ai engineering. For Hasan's work, the useful lens is whether it changes how to build, evaluate, ship, or operate AI-enabled products and agent workflows (20:25).

## Practical Takeaways

- If you're building and shipping a product for people to use, you are part of the design process.
- Design decisions influence basically everything a user feels as they're interacting with all of your products.
- In the age of AI, where prompt and vibe coding is like a very big thing now, like feature parity is no longer like a product differentiator.
- In this talk, we'll walk through what my mental model is design is and hopefully you guys take some stuff away from it.
- You can basically think of speed as like you want your product experiences to feel so fast where like the user does not feel slowed down by it.
