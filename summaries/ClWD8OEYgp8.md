## Practical Takeaways

- This is like a wall of terminal-based coding agents all running in parallel on one person's machine.
- Um alignment has always been a bottleneck, but agents have made the cost of not being aligned as a team much, much higher.
- Unhelpfully, most coding agents also have this local plan mode that is completely unshared with other people.
- None of our current tools give teams a shared space to discuss plans, gather the right context, and work with agents as a collective.
- We need tools that help everyone on the team align before the agents start working, not after.
