## Practical Takeaways

- So, internally, our most curious engineers were some of the very first in the industry to use these types of coding agents.
- In the simplest of terms, I defined an agentic engineering org as one where engineers leverage AI agents as their primary means of producing engineering outcomes.
- So, that meant that our engineers needed to treat agents as core members of their engineering workflow.
- Not just using AI to help them write code here and there, but actually working with agents.
- This model measures the engineer's relationship with AI agents.
