## Practical Takeaways

- They get the feature the way they want it and then they go ahead and they move it to the outer loop where it's tested, reviewed, merged, deployed.
- We were seeing within our own organization a lot of developers adopting AI tools.
- It can make mistakes and almost more scarily it can make security vulnerabilities.
- But we were also seeing our developers suddenly have to review higher volumes of code, test higher volumes of code, merge higher volumes of code, and deploy higher volumes of code.
- Maybe it's background agents, maybe it's reviewers, maybe it's a whole lot of teammates to the workflow.
